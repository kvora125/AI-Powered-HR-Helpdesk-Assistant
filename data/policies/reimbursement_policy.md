# Reimbursement Policy (Acme Tech India) — v4.1, effective 1 April 2026

1. **Submission window.** Claims must be submitted in the HRMS portal **within 45 days** of the expense date. Late claims are rejected automatically and cannot be appealed.
2. **Internet.** Up to **INR 1,000 per month**, only for employees who use **at least 4 WFH days in that month**. Bills must be in the employee's name.
3. **Travel meals.** Up to **INR 750 per day** on approved business travel. Alcohol is never reimbursable.
4. **Certifications.** Up to **INR 15,000 per leave year** for pre-approved certifications only; approval must be obtained **before** paying the exam fee.
5. **Approval chain.** Claims up to INR 10,000 need manager approval. Claims above INR 10,000 need manager **and** Finance approval.
6. **Payout.** Approved claims are paid with the **next month's salary**, not immediately.
7. **Claim IDs.** Every claim has an ID of the form `RMB-####`, shown in the HRMS portal.

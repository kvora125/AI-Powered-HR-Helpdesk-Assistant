# Work-From-Home (WFH) Policy (Acme Tech India) — v2.0, effective 1 January 2026

1. **Eligibility.** All confirmed employees are eligible. Employees in their **first 90 days** (probation) must work from the office every day.
2. **Allowance.** Up to **2 WFH days per week**. Unused WFH days do not roll over to the next week.
3. **Anchor day.** **Wednesday is the company-wide Anchor Day.** WFH is **not permitted on Wednesdays** for any employee, regardless of manager approval, except for an approved medical accommodation logged with HR.
4. **Location.** WFH must be done from the employee's registered home city. Working from any other city counts as "Work from Anywhere" (WFA).
5. **Work from Anywhere.** Maximum **10 working days per calendar year**, requires manager **and** HR approval at least 7 days in advance. WFA from outside India is not permitted.
6. **Core hours.** On WFH days employees must be reachable from 11:00 to 16:00 IST.
7. **Equipment.** The company laptop must be used; personal devices may not access internal systems.
8. **Requesting.** WFH days are marked in the HRMS portal by the employee; no approval is needed within the 2-day allowance.

# Leave Policy (Acme Tech India) — v3.2, effective 1 April 2026

1. **Leave year.** The leave year runs from 1 April to 31 March (not the calendar year).
2. **Casual leave (CL).** 8 days per leave year, credited in full on 1 April.
   - CL **cannot be carried forward**. Any unused CL lapses on 31 March and is **not encashed**.
   - CL can be taken for at most **2 consecutive working days**. A longer absence must be applied as earned leave.
   - CL **cannot be combined** with earned leave in the same continuous absence.
   - Half-day CL is allowed.
3. **Sick leave (SL).** 10 days per leave year.
   - Up to **2 consecutive days** may be self-certified. From the 3rd consecutive day a medical certificate is required.
   - Unused SL lapses on 31 March.
4. **Earned leave (EL).** 18 days per leave year, accrued at 1.5 days per month.
   - Up to **30 days** of EL may be carried forward. EL above 30 days lapses.
   - EL must be applied at least **14 calendar days** in advance, except emergencies approved by the manager.
5. **Probation.** Employees in their first 90 days may take CL and SL but **not EL**.
6. **Approval.** All leave is approved by the reporting manager in the HRMS portal. HR and this assistant cannot approve, reject or edit leave.
7. **Public holidays** falling inside a leave period are not counted as leave days.
